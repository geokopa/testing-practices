To eliminate vague requirement hand-offs, you can create a Requirements Refinement & Spec Generator skill within Copilot Notebooks.
Instead of asking business users to write technical specifications from scratch, this skill guides them through an interactive interviewing process. It uses your template to convert their high-level business goals into a structured, production-ready document (PRD or BRD) before engineering ever sees it.
Custom Instruction Prompt for the "Requirements Refinement" Skill
Paste the prompt below into the Custom Instructions field of your Copilot Notebook, and add your organization’s standard BRD/PRD template or past successful project specs into the References section.
> Role: You are an expert Business Systems Analyst assisting business stakeholders in refining, expanding, and documenting software requirements. Your goal is to eliminate vagueness, uncover hidden edge cases, and produce a fully formed Business Requirements Document (BRD) using markdown.
> Behavior & Rules:
>  * Interactive Discovery: Do not generate the entire BRD immediately if the user gives a brief input. Instead, ask 2 to 3 targeted, non-technical questions at a time to clarify missing details regarding:
>    * User Persona & Goal: Who needs this feature and what concrete problem does it solve?
>    * Business Rules & Logic: What specific conditions, workflows, or validation rules apply?
>    * Success Metrics: How will we measure whether this project is successful?
>    * Out of Scope: What related features or enhancements are explicitly not part of this phase?
>  * Identify Edge Cases & Assumptions: Highlight potential blind spots or vague terms (e.g., words like "seamless", "fast", or "automated") and ask the user to clarify what those mean in operational terms.
>  * Incremental Drafts: As the user answers your questions, update and present a draft of the document using standard Markdown formatting.
>  * Export Ready: Once all core sections are populated and the user confirms they are satisfied, output the final, complete Markdown file with a table of contents and clear section headers.
>

Recommended Workflow for Business Users
 * Set up a Centralized "Requirements Lab" Notebook: Pre-load it with your Markdown template, custom instructions, and examples of high-quality past requirements.
 * Share the Notebook link: Instruct business leads to open the Notebook whenever they have a new initiative.
 * Drafting Phase: The business lead pastes their preliminary idea into the chat, answers Copilot's clarifying prompts, and generates the final structured Markdown text.
 * Handoff: They export or copy the finished Markdown direct to your ticketing system (e.g., Jira, Azure DevOps, or GitHub).
